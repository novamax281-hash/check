Sign in

Your enrollment number is the key.

There's no password to remember and nothing to sign up for. If you're on the college rolls and your mobile number matches our records, you're already on the list.

1Enter your enrollment number and registered mobile number.
2We email a six-digit OTP to the address on file.
3Enter it, and you land on the dashboard for your role.

Wrong mobile number showing against your name? Don't retry — ask the placement cell to correct it first. The portal won't let a mismatched number through, by design.
